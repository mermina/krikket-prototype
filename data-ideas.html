Five ways to show your matches

Your scorers already record every match in CricHeroes. These are five ways the new website could turn that data into something fans want to explore. Example data All fixtures, results and points below are invented to show the idea.

1 Season calendar2 Results grid3 League race4 Head-to-head5 National team journey

1Season calendar

The whole summer at a glance: every match as a dot, coloured by competition. Click a dot for the match.

Data needed: the fixture list (date, teams, competition). Works even before CricHeroes is connected.

2Season results grid

Every club against every other club. Green = the row club won, red = it lost. Fills in as the season goes.

Data needed: results per match.

3League race

Points week by week: watch who pulls ahead. Click a club in the legend to highlight it.

Data needed: results + the league's points rules.

4Head-to-head

Pick two clubs and see every meeting.

Data needed: all past results.
